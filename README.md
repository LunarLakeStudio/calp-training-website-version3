# CALP Training Website Version3

Use this page and take their brand logo and content  https://www.calpnetwork.org/ and https://www.calpnetwork.org/training/calp-training-how-it-works/- and create a modern, sleek and manumatic interactive website CALP Training Hub. Make it modern with the best practice and using the following structure: 1 Sitemap

•     Home — introduction to CALP, highlighted courses, upcoming trainings, language switcher.

•     Courses — the full catalogue. Each course card shows its title and a short summary; filterable by topic/country/language.

–    Course detail page — full description, downloadable free materials (PDFs, guides, videos), and a list of all upcoming Trainings for that course with dates and venues.

•     Calendar — a public, filterable calendar of every upcoming training across all courses and countries. Dates populate automatically the moment a trainer creates a training in the app — no manual website updates needed.

–    Filters: by country, by course, by month and by language.

–    Clicking a date opens that Training's detail page.

•     Training detail / Apply page — training-specific information (description, dates, duration, venue, application deadline, trainer name, language the training is delivered in) plus the multilingual application form.

•     About CALP — organisation background, mission, contact details.

•     News / Updates — optional — space for announcements (can be phased in later).

•     Contact — general enquiry form.
